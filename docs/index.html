My Car TV

My Car TV is a personal iPhone app for watching IPTV streams and YouTube on a CarPlay screen while parked.

What it does

  • Plays M3U playlists and Xtream Codes live TV and movies that the user provides.
  • Lets the user sign in with Google to browse their own YouTube subscriptions, playlists and liked videos, and to search YouTube.
  • Plays YouTube videos with YouTube's official embedded player.
  • Pauses video on the car screen when the vehicle is moving.

This is a private app distributed only through TestFlight to its developer. It is not available on the App Store.
