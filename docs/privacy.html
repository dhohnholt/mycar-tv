Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

My Car TV ("the app") is a personal iPhone app. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled. In short: everything stays on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Google and YouTube data

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests read-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly scope). It uses that access only to:

  • list your subscriptions, playlists and liked videos, and
  • search YouTube on your behalf,

so you can choose videos to watch. The app cannot post, comment, like, subscribe or change anything in your account.

Your Google sign-in tokens are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. They are not synced to iCloud or included in backups. YouTube data is requested directly from Google's servers by your iPhone and is kept only in memory while the app is running. It is never stored on a server, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos are played with the YouTube embedded player, which is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

IPTV playlists and logins

Playlist URLs and Xtream Codes logins you enter are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. They are sent only to the IPTV server you entered, to load your channels and streams.

Location

While CarPlay is connected, the app can read your speed from GPS so it can pause video on the car screen when the vehicle is moving. Location data is used on the device in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No accounts or servers run by the developer.
  • No sharing or selling of any data.

Removing your data

Sign out of YouTube in the app's Settings to delete your tokens and revoke the app's access. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub page.
